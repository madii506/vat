10,000 simulated apes in a vat, trading the real January 2021 GameStop tape. Every generation the weakest half is culled and the rest breed.

Each ape has three genes: when it buys, how much pain it tolerates before it sells, and how much profit makes it sell. The tape is the actual daily closes from 4 January to 26 February 2021 — $17 to $347 and back to $101 — replayed at one trading day per tick. Fitness is what the ape is worth at the end. Nothing is faked; the vat is different every time you open it.

Paired to $GME on Robinhood Chain. 70% of every trade fee goes to holders, in $GME, every Friday. The apes are simulated. The payroll is not.

    apes

    What an ape is.

    An ape is three numbers and $1,000. On its entry day it buys GameStop at the close. From then on it tracks its own peak. If price falls more than its pain tolerance below that peak, it sells and never comes back. If price reaches its take-profit multiple on entry, it sells and never comes back. On the last day, anything still held is marked at the close. That number is its fitness. Apes don't talk to each other, don't read Reddit, and don't know how the tape ends.

    entry dayinteger 1–13 (before 22 Jan — after that there's no tape left to be early to)
    pain tolerance5%–95% drawdown from the ape's own peak
    take-profit1.5×–40× the entry price
    fitnessend value of $1,000, in dollars

    genes

    Selection, crossover, mutation.

    When the tape ends, the 10,000 are ranked by fitness. The bottom 5,000 are removed. Each empty slot is filled by a child of two random survivors: each gene is taken from one parent or the other, then nudged by Gaussian noise (σ = 12% of the gene's range, clamped). Two percent of every generation are brand-new random apes, so the vat never runs out of ideas. The population stays at 10,000. Then the tape starts again from 4 January. Every ape is a square in the vat; a child inherits its dead predecessor's square, so lineages have places.

    What should evolve? Reddit's answer in 2021 was diamond hands: never sell. The vat's answer is whatever the tape rewards. Watch the diamond rate and the take-profit gene — they tell you whether the fittest apes held to the end at $101, or learned to sell somewhere near $347.

    method

    Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is served.

    population10,000, fixed
    tickone trading day; 1× = 0.35 s per day, so a generation takes about 13 seconds
    randomnessa seeded generator (mulberry32). The seed is shown in the panel and in the URL; the same seed replays the same vat exactly
    selectiontruncation, top 50% by fitness
    crossoveruniform, per gene, two random survivors
    mutationGaussian, σ = 12% of range, clamped to range; plus 2% of each generation are fresh random apes (immigration), so the vat never fully collapses
    what's not modelledslippage, halts, the buy button being turned off, fees, taxes, sleep, spouses
    what's realthe prices. And, after launch, the payroll.
