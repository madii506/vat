    The rule

    Each ape is three numbers. The day it buys, somewhere in the first thirteen trading days of 2021. The multiple of its buy price at which it takes profit, between 1.5× and 40×. And the fall from its own high it will sit through before it bails, between 5% and 95%. It puts $1,000 into GameStop at the close of its day and holds until one of its two numbers is hit or the tape ends on 26 February. It does not read the news. It does not know how the tape ends.

    When the tape ends, the 10,000 are ranked by what their $1,000 became. The poorer half is removed. Every empty place goes to a child of two survivors: each number comes from one parent or the other, nudged by a little noise. Two in a hundred are strangers with random numbers. Then it is 4 January again.

    What is real and what is ours

    Real. The tape: 38 trading days of GameStop from 4 January to 26 February 2021, open, high, low, close and volume as they printed, with the 2022 split undone so that 27 January closes at $347.51 and 28 January's high is $483. Today's price of $GME, looked up once a minute. The pair: $GME stock tokens on Robinhood Chain, through Pons.

    Ours. The ape: three numbers and nothing else, no news, no forum, no memory of the last run. The breeding: half die, the rest cross, noise at 12% of each number's range, 2% strangers. The places: every ape has a fixed square in the vat and a child takes its dead predecessor's square, so lineages have addresses. The desk: a $10,000 book with no wallet behind it yet, so no real order is placed; it lives in your browser, one per seed. And the payroll: 70% of every 1% trade fee, paid in $GME to holders each Friday.

    Thesis

    In January 2021 a video game retailer went from $17 to $483 in nineteen trading days because a few million people decided not to sell. Then most of them did. The ones who held are remembered. The ones who sold at the top are not, because nobody admits to it.

    vat asks the tape what it actually paid for. Ten thousand strategies, no memory, no forum, bred on outcome alone. After a few generations the answer is on the strip at the top of this page: the vat sells. It sells near $347, and it bails early on the way down. Diamond hands do not survive selection. That is not an opinion; it is what the numbers do when nobody is watching them.

    The coin is paired to $GME on Robinhood Chain. Every trade pays 1%; seven tenths of it goes to holders, in $GME, every Friday. The apes were never paid for holding. You are.
